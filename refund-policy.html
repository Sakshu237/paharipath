REFUND & CANCELLATION POLICY

PahariPath
Operated by: Saksham, a sole proprietorship trading as "PahariPath"
Effective Date: 27-10-2026 | Last Updated: 27-10-2026

This policy explains what happens when a Booking made on paharipath.in is cancelled, and how refunds are paid. It forms part of our Terms of Service (Clause 6).

1. Cancelling a Booking

You can cancel any time before check-in from My Profile → My Bookings, or with Manage / Cancel Booking in the site footer using your booking reference and phone number. Before you confirm, the app always shows the exact cancellation fee and refund amount.

2. Cancellation fee schedule

Check-in time is taken as 12:00 noon on the check-in date.

When you cancel Cancellation fee Refund of the stay amount
48 hours or more before check-in 0% 100%
Between 48 hours and 3 hours before check-in Rises evenly from 0% to 100% Falls evenly from 100% to 0%
Less than 3 hours before check-in, or after check-in 100% None

The stay amount includes any GST charged on it. Carbon offset contributions are non-refundable once a Booking is confirmed (Terms of Service, Clause 5.3).

3. "Pay at the stay" bookings

If you chose to pay at the stay, nothing was charged online, so there is nothing to refund. Please still cancel through the Platform so the Host can re-open the dates.

4. How refunds are paid

  • Refunds are made only to the original payment method used on Razorpay (card, UPI, net banking or wallet).
  • We start the refund automatically as soon as you cancel. Banks usually credit it within 5–7 working days.
  • If you have not received it after 7 working days, write to hello@paharipath.in with your booking reference.

5. If the Host cancels, or the dates are no longer available

If a Host cancels a confirmed Booking, or the dates were taken while your payment was being processed, you get a full refund of everything you paid, including any carbon offset contribution. We will also try to help you find a comparable Stay.

6. Events outside anyone's control

For landslides, road closures, heavy snow, official travel restrictions or similar events, the Terms of Service (Clause 6.2) apply. Contact us and we will work with you and the Host on a fair outcome, such as new dates.
